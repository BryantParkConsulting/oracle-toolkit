# BPC Customer Assessment — Service Overview

**Target duration:** approximately 2 minutes

Bryant Park Consulting's Customer Assessment gives organizations a clear, evidence-based view of their NetSuite and Oracle NSPB environments.

It answers three practical questions: What is installed? What is actually being used? And what should improve next?

The client provides secure, read-only NetSuite access and an NSPB backup. Once those inputs are received, BPC can typically complete the assessment within one business day.

For NSPB, we inventory dimensions, forms, business rules, cubes, dashboards, integrations, scheduled jobs, security, and data volume. We then classify each capability as active, partially used, dormant, or no longer relevant.

For NetSuite, we compare enabled modules with actual transaction activity. We evaluate the chart of accounts, customizations, scripts, connected applications, and readiness for Planning.

This distinction matters. A configured feature does not prove that the business uses it. The assessment connects configuration with operational evidence, so clients can see what is working, what is inactive, and where adoption has stalled.

The performance review identifies stale scenarios, oversized data footprints, slow calculations, fragile rules, navigation issues, and opportunities to simplify the model.

Recommendations are tailored to the client's industry and operating model. They explain what to enable, consolidate, clean up, retire, govern differently, or reinforce through training.

Each technical finding is translated into business impact: faster planning cycles, more reliable reporting, lower maintenance effort, stronger adoption, and greater flexibility.

The final delivery includes an executive current-state assessment, a performance review, a NetSuite module analysis, and a prioritized technical and functional action plan.

This service is especially valuable when an organization is returning to NSPB after a period of low usage, preparing for a new budget cycle, or deciding where to invest next.

You provide the secure inputs. Bryant Park Consulting turns them into clarity, priorities, and a practical roadmap.
