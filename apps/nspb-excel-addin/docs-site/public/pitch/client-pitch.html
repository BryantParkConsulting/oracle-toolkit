08 · Setup & Security
Set up in minutes

No new infrastructure. No new logins.
No data leaves your control.

Step 1 · Install
One-click via Microsoft 365.

Distributed as a shared Office add-in through your admin center. No desktop install on user machines.

Step 2 · Connect
Three fields and a click.

Your NSPB host, your existing login, and a Gemini key. “Test connections” validates everything in one go.

Step 3 · Security
Stateless. Your credentials, your tenant.
  • ✓ The assistant calls NSPB with your credentials — same permissions, same audit trail.
  • ✓ No copy of your data is stored. Stateless by design.
  • ✓ Bring your own Gemini key, or use one provided by NSPB MCP.
